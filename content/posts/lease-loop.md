A leasing app built for the Northfina × Hive Helsinki hackathon. My focus was the Money Core: saved quotes, agreed contract pricing, and invoices that explain VAT changes.

## My contribution

Lease Loop was a team project for the Northfina × Hive Helsinki hackathon. I contributed specifically to the Money Core: saving the original quote, checking the final price at signing, and recording the agreed rent, buyout, and dealer discount.

A key concern in this part of the system is keeping the agreed monthly net rent consistent while explaining changes in invoice totals caused by VAT.

## Why it matters

Invoices can explain VAT changes while preserving the agreed monthly net rent. The project uses domain functions and scenario tests to check the lease journey.
